# King Doe reading list - books Chris Do recommends in the corpus

Every entry has the video and timestamp where it comes up, his words (verbatim ASR, lightly trimmed), and a legitimate purchase or borrow link (publisher, author, or mainstream bookstore). No unauthorized PDF sources.

Coverage note (v2): this list is based on a keyword scan of ALL 89 transcripts (book/read/author/wrote/recommend patterns), with every hit read in context and classified into tiers:

- **Explicitly recommended (18)** - he tells the audience to read or buy it.
- **Taught from / positively referenced (4)** - he builds a lesson on it or praises it, but never says "go read it."
- **Guest endorsement (1)** - the book is his guest's own, and he tells the audience to pick it up.
- **Mentioned, not shelved** - it comes up without a recommendation.
- Guest self-promotions he does not endorse are excluded (listed at the end with timestamps).

ASR errors are possible; the video+timestamp lets you verify any entry in seconds.

## Tier 1: Explicitly recommended

### Core sales books

1. Socratic Selling - Kevin Daley
   "the five biggest lessons I've learned by reading Socratic Selling... If you're an introvert like myself and hate sales, this is your key. Read this book. It's under 200 pages." (NQT1lgXPPHs [00:00], [13:35]; also credited in the interview at 67J7070mwFg [05:20])

   "Hot Take: The Coaching Habit is one of the best sales books that you can buy... be slower to give advice, be curious for a little bit longer."
