## Evidence basis - read this before trusting the buckets

- Topic assignment is based on video titles and hand fixes, cross-checked against a keyword theme scan (7 themes, 394 timestamped excerpt hits) that directly touched 78 of the 89 videos. It is NOT a full-text re-read of every transcript. The interview (lesson 01) and the two book videos were read in full; the rest were classified from titles plus the scan hits. A video can teach more than its title advertises, so treat buckets as entry points, not boundaries; when the actual content matters, verify against the video at the cited timestamp instead of trusting the bucket.
- Word counts come from the local transcript files, so they are exact for those files, but they measure raw ASR captions (including intros, ads, and crosstalk), not teaching density.
- Book evidence is separate and stronger: READING-LIST.md is a keyword scan over all 89 transcripts with every hit read in context.
