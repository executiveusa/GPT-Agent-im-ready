# The Council

A council of advisory seats - research-based personas inspired by public
thinkers - that an orchestrator can consult for plans, PRDs and decisions. Each
seat is a set of documents: a profile (role, principles, questions, limits)
where one exists, and a source manifest (SOURCES.md) listing the public videos
or books the seat is distilled from.

Source transcripts are NOT in this repo. They are third-party content; the
owner keeps them in his own Google Drive under his own sharing settings, and
they are not republished here or in the open layer.

## Open reference layer (agent-readable)

The same reference layer is in an open, anyone-with-link Google Drive folder

https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1teAYKPJD6Gs6VD45oxyU7Z8Pgwy6C21a

## Boundaries

- Seats are advisory lenses, not the real people, and not their endorsement.
- This repo and the open reference layer contain indexes, notes and short cited
  excerpts only; full transcripts and book texts are not republished.
- Private individuals under restriction are excluded entirely.
- No seat publishes, sends, spends or promises without the owner's approval.
